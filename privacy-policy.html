AI Crop Intelligence Platform — Privacy Policy

📅 Effective date: June 29, 2026 🔄 Last updated: June 29, 2026 🇮🇳 Applicable: India (IT Act 2000 & PDPB compliant)
Your privacy matters to us. KrishiKonnect ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the personal information of farmers, agronomists, and researchers who use our platform. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. We do not sell your data. Ever.
1

Who We Are

KrishiKonnect is an AI-powered crop intelligence platform designed for Indian farmers and agricultural professionals. We provide crop disease diagnosis, APMC mandi price data, weather advisories, and an AI chat assistant for agricultural queries.

This privacy policy applies to the KrishiKonnect web application at krishi-konnect-app-odl2.vercel.app and any associated Android application available on the Google Play Store.

3

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the purposes stated below:

  • To generate your personalised crop diagnostic report and farm advisory
  • To power the KrishiAI chatbot with context-relevant agricultural answers
  • To show APMC mandi prices relevant to your region
  • To send service emails (account confirmation, password reset)
  • To improve our AI models and app features using anonymised, aggregated data
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for commercial sale, or sharing with third-party marketers.

4

AI Processing & Field Images Important

When you upload a field image for crop diagnosis, the image is sent to our AI pipeline (powered by Anthropic Claude and other AI models) for visual analysis. This processing identifies disease patterns, lesions, and symptom indicators.

  • Images are processed in real time and are not stored permanently on our servers unless you explicitly save a case report
  • Saved case images are retained for 90 days and then automatically deleted
  • Your images are never used to train external AI models without your explicit written consent
  • AI-generated diagnoses are for advisory purposes only — always consult a certified agronomist for critical decisions

6

Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) — never plain HTTP
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords
  • User accounts are protected with email-verified authentication
  • Our hosting infrastructure (Vercel) complies with SOC 2 Type II security standards
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our codebase and dependencies
⚠️ Reporting a vulnerability If you discover a security issue in KrishiKonnect, please report it responsibly to our email below. We will respond within 48 hours.
7

Your Rights & Choices

As a KrishiKonnect user, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correct — update incorrect or outdated information in your profile
  • Delete — request complete deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of non-essential data processing at any time
  • Data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
  • Object — object to processing of your data for specific purposes

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@krishikonnect.in. We will respond within 30 days as required under Indian law.

8

Cookies & Local Storage

KrishiKonnect uses minimal browser storage to make the app work:

  • Session storage — keeps you signed in during your visit (cleared when you close the browser)
  • Local storage — saves your language preference and selected crop region
  • No third-party tracking cookies — we do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking tools

You can clear all stored data by signing out, or by clearing your browser's site data for our domain.

9

Children's Privacy

KrishiKonnect is designed for farmers, agronomists, and agricultural professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Continued use of KrishiKonnect after a policy update means you accept the updated terms.
